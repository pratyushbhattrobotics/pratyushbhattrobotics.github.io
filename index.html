01 / LAB RESEARCH

Hexapod Locomotion Research

Studying locomotion efficiency by comparing alternating tripod gait patterns on a six-legged robot.

Research Assistant
Locomotion in Biology and Robotics Lab, Penn State

Legged robotics Vicon Nexus Gait analysis Mechanical design
My contributions
  • Collecting and analyzing experimental data across different phase shifts and duty cycles to evaluate gait performance.
  • Assisting in constructing and modifying the hexapod, including designing mechanical adjustments.
  • Calibrating sensors and implementing optimized gait patterns during lab testing.
02 / INDEPENDENT PROJECT

4-DOF Robotic Arm

Developing a servo-driven desktop arm that connects mechanical construction with ROS 2 simulation and motion planning.

Arduino Uno ROS 2 Jazzy MoveIt 2 Gazebo
Project scope
  • Assemble and calibrate base, shoulder, elbow, and gripper servos.
  • Configure the robot model, simulation, and motion-planning workflow.
  • Develop a USB serial connection between the ROS 2 computer and Arduino.
  • Extend the project toward webcam-based perception using OpenCV.
03 / IN DEVELOPMENT

Self-Balancing Robot

Building a two-wheeled robot to explore how inertial sensing and PID feedback can maintain an upright position.

PID control MPU6050 Arduino Uno 3D printing
Project goals
  • Build a chassis and integrate motors, a motor driver, an IMU, and battery-powered electronics.
  • Estimate tilt from inertial measurements.
  • Implement and tune a PID controller, then evaluate balancing performance.
04 / EARLY DEVELOPMENT

Handheld Visual SLAM

Exploring camera-based indoor mapping and trajectory estimation using a webcam and an Ubuntu laptop.

Visual SLAM ROS 2 Jazzy Logitech C270 Ubuntu
Planned evaluation
  • Calibrate the camera and collect indoor video data.
  • Integrate a monocular SLAM pipeline for trajectory estimation and sparse mapping.
  • Test place recognition and loop closure by returning to previously visited locations.
  • Investigate scale ambiguity and compare reconstructed geometry against measured room dimensions after establishing scale.
